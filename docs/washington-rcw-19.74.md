# State Electronic Transactions Laws

Washington's RCW 19.74 and Missouri's Uniform Electronic Transactions Act
(Mo. Rev. Stat. §§ 432.200–432.295) address the legal effect of electronic
records, electronic signatures, and electronic contracts in transactions
within their respective scopes.
