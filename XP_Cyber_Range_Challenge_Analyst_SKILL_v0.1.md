# 1. Purpose

Operate effectively within the XP Cyber Range and NICE Challenge environment.

This skill is designed to help a learner:
- access the correct workspace and assignment;
- determine whether a workspace is available, scheduled, or expired;
- start a challenge at the correct time;
- complete challenge activities using approved methods;
- collect evidence and screenshots;
- submit proof of completion in Canvas;
- avoid common workflow mistakes that block scoring or submission.

This is a practical operational skill, not only a conceptual summary.

# 6. Core Workflow Sequence

Use the following operational order:

`ACCESS -> VERIFY ASSIGNMENT -> CHECK WORKSPACE STATUS -> SCHEDULE OR OPEN -> START CHALLENGE -> COMPLETE -> SUBMIT -> CAPTURE EVIDENCE -> REPORT`

## Step 1: Access the XP Cyber Range / NICE Challenge web portal
1. Log in using the assigned institutional or platform account.
2. Confirm that the learner is registered for the correct course or assignment.
3. Confirm that the correct challenge or course page is visible after login.
4. If the learner cannot see an assignment, confirm proper account access and instructor assignment status.

## Step 2: Verify workspace access and assignment status
Players must be added to an assignment by a curator before they can access a workspace.

Check for:
- active workspaces;
- pending scheduling;
- expired or no-longer-available workspaces;
- assignment not yet visible.

The curator determines:
- which challenge(s) may be attempted;
- how long the workspace is available;
- whether the learner is eligible to schedule a workspace.

## Step 3: Understand workspace timing
Workspaces typically become available at approximately 3:00 AM Pacific Time on the designated start date.

Once a workspace is available, the learner sees an "Open Workspace" button.

Workspaces expire at 11:59 PM Pacific Time on the designated end date. Any challenge attempt still in progress is automatically submitted at expiration.

## Step 4: Schedule a workspace if required
A player must be in a Player-Scheduled Assignment before scheduling a workspace for themselves.

If the assignment is pending scheduling, the platform may display:
- "Not Scheduled" in red under the assignment date fields;
- workspace start and end dates as unset.

The learner must:
1. choose the assignment;
2. select the desired scheduling window within the authorized assignment phase;
3. confirm the workspace dates;
4. avoid scheduling outside the permitted assignment phase.

## Step 5: Open the workspace
Once the workspace is active, select the challenge or assignment entry and open the workspace.

Action: choose the available workspace and proceed to challenge selection or deployment.

## Step 6: Start the challenge
After entering the workspace, the learner must:
- select the challenge to attempt;
- launch or deploy the challenge environment;
- confirm the environment is ready before beginning task work;
- avoid unnecessary resets unless the challenge instructions require them.

The challenge may have time-sensitive steps or a required sequence. Follow the challenge brief and assignment instructions carefully.

## Step 7: Complete the challenge using approved methods
Use the platform, provided tools, research, and course knowledge to complete the assignment.

Best practice:
- understand the task before acting;
- identify the security objective and constraints;
- maintain operational notes and screenshots;
- use approved research tools when needed;
- do not attempt unauthorized bypasses or exploit steps not allowed by the assessment.

If a challenge requires a technical or analytical process, document:
- objective;
- method used;
- result or evidence;
- any assumptions or constraints.

## Step 8: Submit the challenge
When the challenge is complete, use the platform's submission workflow.

Before submitting, check that:
- required evidence is available;
- the challenge output is complete;
- the final state reflects the actual task result;
- no submission is left incomplete or partially performed.

If the environment auto-submits unfinished attempts when the workspace expires, make sure the learner is not relying on late completion without prior confirmation.

## Step 9: Review challenge submissions
After submission, the learner may view challenge submissions to verify:
- which attempt was submitted;
- whether the challenge completed successfully;
- whether there is a record of final status;
- whether screenshots or proof are attached or needed.

Use this review step to confirm the work was actually recorded and not lost due to timing, submission error, or incomplete state.

# 7. Evidence Capture Rules

Collect evidence throughout the workflow.

Minimum evidence set:
- login confirmation;
- assignment/workspace visibility;
- workspace scheduling or open workspace screen;
- challenge initiation screen;
- challenge environment or task screen;
- final challenge result or completion state;
- submission confirmation;
- Canvas screenshot showing what is being submitted for grading.

Evidence rule:
- if it is not captured and visible, assume it may not be accepted for grading.

# 9. Challenge Tips and Common Questions

## Challenge Tips
- Check the assignment early and verify that the workspace has been assigned.
- Schedule the workspace within the valid assignment window.
- Start the challenge only when the environment is ready and available.
- Keep screenshots throughout the process; do not wait until the end.
- Validate that the challenge is actually submitted before assuming completion.
- Be aware of timezone issues, especially Pacific Time windows.
- Do not assume the environment remains open after the deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What if I cannot see a workspace?
Confirm that the curator has added the player to the assignment and that the assignment window is active.

### What if the workspace is not scheduled?
The learner may need to complete scheduling within the assignment phase before the workspace becomes available.

### What if the challenge is still in progress when the deadline ends?
The platform may auto-submit in-progress attempts. Do not rely on a last-minute workflow without confirmation.

### What if I need help?
Use the platform support channel, course resources, and assignment instructions. Clarify with the curator or instructor when the issue is access-specific or assignment-specific.

# 10. Console Support and Availability

Support and environment availability can vary. If the challenge or platform appears unavailable:
- verify the assignment is active;
- check whether the workspace is scheduled and open;
- confirm not outside the window;
- review any console or availability notices;
- contact the appropriate support route or instructor if the issue is not resolved.

Do not treat a platform outage as a failure of the learner. Follow the assignment path and record the issue clearly if required.

# 11. Research and AI Use Guidance

The course allows research and the use of GenAI or other tools as long as they support learning and challenge completion in a legitimate way.

Use AI and research responsibly:
- validate information against the challenge requirements;
- prefer authoritative sources and assigned materials;
- do not use prohibited exploit or bypass guidance;
- stay within the rules of the environment and academic integrity policy;
- document what supplemental research was needed and what conclusions were derived.

This skill helps convert research into operational execution and submission evidence.

# 12. Decision Rules

Choose the next action based on state:

- If the learner is not assigned to a workspace: resolve access/curator issue before starting.
- If the workspace is pending scheduling: schedule within the permitted window.
- If the workspace is active: open it and launch the challenge.
- If the challenge is in progress: proceed with evidence capture and task completion.
- If the challenge is complete: submit and verify submission status.
- If the environment or access is broken: document the issue and escalate through the proper support channel.

# 13. Failure/Stop Conditions

Stop and escalate when:
- the learner is not yet added to the assignment;
- the platform denies login or workspace access;
- scheduling is not authorized or outside the assignment phase;
- the challenge environment fails to deploy or launch;
- the learner cannot distinguish between a task requirement and a prohibited action;
- the learner is unsure whether a method violates the challenge rules or academic policy.
